# Next directions

Everything deliberately left for later, in planned versions, so every "not yet" says exactly how. The `record-decision` skill adds a row when a decision defers something; any role may propose one; the founder reorders. The dashboard's Roadmap shows it as a version rail.

**Why later** is one of three reasons, never blurred:
- **not possible**: it can't be done with today's tools or rules (say what would have to change)
- **needs a credential**: possible now, but waits on an account, key or approval the founder hasn't given
- **out of scope**: possible, deliberately kept out of the current version

Version is `v1`, `v2`, ... in order, or `always` for a habit rather than a release.
