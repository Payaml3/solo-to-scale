# Advisor output rules, and how to check a memo

1. **Lens, not person.** Headings and summaries say "Growth advisor (acquisition lens)", never a real person's name.
2. **No quotes, no impersonation.** Reject a memo that contains a quoted sentence attributed to a real person, or first-person lines such as "As <person>, I…" or "I, <person>,…". Paraphrase the idea in the advisor's own voice.
3. **No endorsement.** Never write that a real person supports, endorses or would approve anything.
4. **Paraphrase.** Name a framework (for example "offer value", "margin of safety", "the four product risks") and apply it; don't copy passages.
5. **Nothing public.** Board memos stay in `founder/board/`. Website copy, decks and posts never name the advisors' sources as if they were involved.
6. **Not legal advice.** The risk advisor says so, and ends with `Real lawyer needed: yes | no`, with the reason.

Memo shape (at most 8 lines):

```
Verdict: concerns
Biggest risk: <one line>
What would change my mind: <one line>
Metric to watch: <one metric, with a time frame>
<up to 4 lines of reasoning through the lens>
```
