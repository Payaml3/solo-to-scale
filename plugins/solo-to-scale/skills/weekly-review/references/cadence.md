# Monthly and quarterly additions

**Monthly (first Monday):** metrics and evidence, error-budget and unit-cost review (from stage 21), stale Proposed or Open items to decide or close, roadmap check, the month's Claude usage by role.

**Quarterly (start of quarter):** objectives and key results in `founder/goals.json` (the founder sets them; `python tools/goals.py show` lists last quarter's results), a strategy check against the evidence with all four advisors through `board-review`, a Well-Architected review (from stage 27), and an investor or board update built from the views.
